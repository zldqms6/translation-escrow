[Fictional project example] Hanbit Swap Season 2 Liquidity Campaign

The Hanbit Swap Season 2 liquidity campaign starts on October 13 at 10:00 AM KST.

During the campaign, wallets that provide liquidity to the ETH/USDC pool earn points based on how much they supply and for how long. Points are tallied once a day at midnight UTC, and each wallet can earn up to 5,000 points per day.

The campaign runs for four weeks, and the final leaderboard will be published within 7 days after it ends. The top 300 wallets will receive additional rewards.

Please note: providing liquidity carries the risk of impermanent loss. Points are not tokens and do not guarantee any future token allocation. If one person is found to be using multiple wallets, all of those wallets will be removed from the leaderboard.
